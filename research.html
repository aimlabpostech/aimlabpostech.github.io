What We Work On

The AIM Lab conducts research at the intersection of Process Mining, Recommender Systems, and Applied AI. These three core areas are complemented by AI-Driven Process Innovation, Simulation & Digital Twins, and Predictive & Prescriptive Analytics.

Across these areas, we develop methods that transform complex data into process intelligence, personalized recommendations, and AI-driven decisions. Our research combines methodological advances with real-world applications, particularly in manufacturing, healthcare, and the fashion industry.

Process Mining

We develop methods to discover, analyze, predict, and improve real-world processes from event data. Our research spans the process mining lifecycle, from event-data preparation and process discovery to conformance checking, predictive monitoring, simulation, and optimization.

Current research topics include:

  • Object-centric process mining and object-centric process models
  • Event abstraction from low-level event data
  • Process discovery and conformance checking
  • Predictive process monitoring
  • Privacy-aware process mining and event-data partitioning
  • Process model visualization and layout optimization
  • Trace clustering and analysis of heterogeneous process behavior
  • Integration of process mining with AI and machine learning

Our goal is to move beyond simply visualizing processes toward building process intelligence that helps organizations understand what is happening, why it is happening, and how processes can be improved.

Recommender Systems

We develop personalized and context-aware recommendation technologies that help users make better choices from large and complex sets of alternatives.

A major focus of our research is the fashion industry, where we have conducted long-term industry collaborations on personalized product recommendation, outfit recommendation, customer preference modeling, and AI-driven curation.

Our research includes:

  • Personalized fashion product recommendation
  • Outfit and style recommendation
  • User preference and behavioral modeling
  • Context-aware recommendation
  • Customer journey and interaction analytics
  • Content and video recommendation
  • Integration of recommender systems with generative AI and large language models

We aim to develop recommendation technologies that go beyond predicting user preferences to provide meaningful, personalized, and actionable recommendations in real-world services.

Applied AI

We develop and apply advanced artificial intelligence and machine learning methods to solve real-world problems where domain knowledge, complex data, and operational constraints matter.

Our work includes:

  • Generative AI and large language models
  • AI agents and multi-agent systems
  • Domain-specific foundation models
  • Deep learning and representation learning
  • Explainable AI
  • Multimodal AI
  • Intelligent document and data analysis
  • AI for manufacturing and healthcare

Recent research includes manufacturing foundation models, LLM-based event-log extraction, AI agents for process analysis and improvement, and intelligent analysis of complex medical documents.

Our emphasis is not only on developing AI models, but also on making AI useful, reliable, and deployable in real operational environments.

AI-Driven Process Innovation

We study how AI and process intelligence can fundamentally change the way organizations operate.

Rather than using AI only to automate individual tasks, we investigate how processes can be redesigned around AI capabilities, data-driven decision making, and human–AI collaboration.

Our research includes:

  • AI-assisted process redesign and innovation
  • Generative AI and LLM agents for process improvement
  • Identification and generation of process improvement scenarios
  • Human–AI collaboration in business and operational processes
  • AI-enabled workflow automation
  • Integration of process mining and AI for continuous improvement
  • Data-driven evaluation of process redesign alternatives

The goal is to move from analyzing existing processes to designing better processes for the AI era.

Simulation & Digital Twins

We develop data-driven simulation and digital twin technologies that enable organizations to experiment with changes before implementing them in the real world.

Rather than relying solely on manually constructed simulation models, we investigate how models can be generated and continuously updated using event data, process mining, and operational data.

Our research includes:

  • Automated generation of process simulation models
  • Process digital twins
  • Hospital operation digital twins
  • Manufacturing process simulation
  • What-if analysis and scenario evaluation
  • Integration of process mining, simulation, and optimization
  • Data-driven calibration and validation of simulation models

Applications include hospital operations, steel manufacturing, semiconductor processes, and smart manufacturing systems.

Predictive & Prescriptive Analytics

We develop methods that move from understanding what has happened to predicting what will happen and what should be done next.

Our research integrates machine learning, process analytics, and optimization to translate predictions into better operational decisions.

Research topics include:

  • Process performance prediction
  • Deep-learning-based predictive analytics
  • Predictive process monitoring
  • Resource allocation and scheduling
  • Optimization based on predicted process outcomes
  • Operational decision support
  • Prescriptive analytics for process improvement

Our goal is to connect prediction with action, enabling organizations to anticipate problems, evaluate alternatives, and make better decisions.

Application Domains

Our research is motivated by real-world problems and developed through close collaboration with industry and public-sector partners. Three application domains are particularly central to our work.

Manufacturing

We apply process mining, AI, simulation, and optimization to complex manufacturing systems. Research topics include semiconductor process analysis, steel manufacturing simulation, smart factory operations, manufacturing foundation models, process optimization, and AI-supported manufacturing intelligence.

Healthcare

We develop process intelligence and digital twin technologies for healthcare operations, including clinical pathway analysis, outpatient and emergency department processes, hospital digital twins, process simulation, and AI-based analysis of medical data and documents.

Fashion

The fashion industry is a major application domain for our recommender systems research. Through long-term industry collaboration, we develop personalized product recommendation, fashion curation, outfit recommendation, customer preference modeling, and AI-driven fashion services.

From Methods to Impact

The six research areas are closely connected rather than independent. A project may combine process mining with AI, use predictions to drive optimization, construct a digital twin from process data, or integrate recommender systems with generative AI.

This interdisciplinary approach allows us to move from understanding data to discovering processes, predicting outcomes, recommending actions, redesigning operations, and building intelligent systems that create measurable real-world impact.
