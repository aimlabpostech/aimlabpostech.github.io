Dr. Minsu Cho Appointed Professor at Kwangwoon University

Dr. Minsu Cho, who received his Ph.D. from the AIM Lab, was appointed as an assistant professor in the School of Information Convergence at Kwangwoon University in March of this year. We wish him an active and productive research career.
