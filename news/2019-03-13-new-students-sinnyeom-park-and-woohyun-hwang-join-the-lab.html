New Students Sinnyeom Park and Woohyun Hwang Join the Lab (Spring 2019)(박신념, 황우현 학생 입학(2019-1))

Sinnyeom Park and Woohyun Hwang entered the master's program in the spring of 2019, adding two more members to the AIM Lab.

We look forward to a rewarding and energetic graduate school experience for Sinnyeom Park and Woohyun Hwang.
