Spring Research Workshop of the Shipbuilding and Ocean Engineering Production Research Group

2018 Spring Research Workshop of the Shipbuilding and Ocean Engineering Production Research Group

Attendees: Prof. Minseok Song, Jongwon Kim

Jongwon Kim presented the industry-academia research project with Hyundai Heavy Industries, "Visualization of In-Shop Processes and Lead Time Optimization Analysis," and received the Best Presentation Award in the Production Systems session.
