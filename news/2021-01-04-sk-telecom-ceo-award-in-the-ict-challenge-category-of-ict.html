SK Telecom CEO Award in the ICT Challenge Category of ICT Colloquium 2020

In the ICT Challenge category of ICT Colloquium 2020, the project "Sports Gamification Broadcasting Platform" (participating students: Mincheol Jung, Youngseok Jang, Jaemin Shin) received the SK Telecom CEO Award.
