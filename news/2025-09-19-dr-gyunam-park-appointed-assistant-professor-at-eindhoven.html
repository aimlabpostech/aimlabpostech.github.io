Dr. Gyunam Park Appointed Assistant Professor at Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)

Dr. Gyunam Park, an AIM Lab master's program alumnus (advisor: Prof. Minseok Song), was appointed Assistant Professor in the Process Analytics Cluster of the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e) as of July 2025. Dr. Park received his bachelor's degree from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at UNIST and his master's degree from the Department of Industrial and Management Engineering at POSTECH, and earned his Ph.D. in the research group of Prof. Wil van der Aalst, Honorary Director of the POSTECH Wil van der Aalst Data & Process Science Research Center and Professor at RWTH Aachen University in Germany. He also served as a group leader at the Fraunhofer Institute for Applied Information Technology, where he achieved outstanding results in both research and industry-academia collaboration. Prof. Park is expected to continue his active research in process mining and data science and to contribute greatly to the global academic community and to industry.

More information about Prof. Gyunam Park is available at https://www.gyunam.com.

박규남 박사, Eindhoven University of Technology(TU/e) 조교수 부임

AIM랩 석사 졸업생 Gyunam Park 박사(지도교수 Minseok Song)께서 지난 2025년 7월부로 Eindhoven University of Technology(TU/e)의 Process Analytics Cluster in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science에 Assistant Professor(조교수)로 부임하였습니다. 박규남 박사님께서는 UNIST 컴퓨터공학과 학사, POSTECH 산업경영공학과 석사 과정을 거쳐 POSTECH Wil van der Aalst Data & Process Science Research Center의 명예 센터장이자 독일 아헨공대 교수인 Prof. Wil van der Aalst 연구실에서 박사 학위를 취득하셨습니다. 또한, Fraunhofer Institute for Applied Information Technology에서 그룹 리더를 역임하며 연구와 산학 협력에서 탁월한 성과를 거두셨습니다. 앞으로도 박규남 교수님은 프로세스 마이닝(Process Mining) 및 데이터 사이언스(Data Science) 분야에서 활발한 연구 활동을 이어가며, 글로벌 학계와 산업 발전에 크게 기여할 것으로 기대됩니다.
