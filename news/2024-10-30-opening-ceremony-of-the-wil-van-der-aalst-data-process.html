Opening Ceremony of the Wil van der Aalst Data & Process Science Research Center and AP-PM Workshop

On September 24, 2024, Prof. Wil van der Aalst visited POSCO Changeup Ground and delivered a keynote lecture to commemorate the opening of the Wil van der Aalst Data & Process Science Research Center. Prof. Wil van der Aalst is the founder of the field of process mining, and his keynote lecture addressed Object-Centric Process Mining.

Following the opening ceremony, the Asia-Pacific Process Mining Workshop was held from September 24 through September 25. The workshop was attended by members of the Department of Industrial and Management Engineering at POSTECH, along with participants from Celonis Korea, Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance, Hallym University Medical Center, and LG Electronics, as well as professors from UNIST, Kwangwoon University, Kyung Hee University, and Pusan National University.

In addition, on the afternoon of September 25, Prof. Wil van der Aalst held a research seminar with the members of the AIM Lab.

Wil van der Aalst Data & Process Science Research Center 개소식 및 AP-PM 워크숍 진행

2024년 9월 24일에 Wil van der Aalst 교수님께서 포스코 체인지업 그라운드에 방문하셔서 Wil van der Aalst Data & Process Science Research Center 개소식 기념 keynote 강연을 진행하셨습니다. Wil van der Aalst 교수님께서는 Process Mining 분야의 창시자이시며, Object-Centric Process Mining에 대한 keynote 강연을 진행해주셨습니다.

개소식 이후에는 9월 24일에 이어 25일까지 Asia-Pacific Process Mining 워크샵을 진행하였습니다. 워크샵에는 포항공대 산경과 구성원과 Celonis Korea, 삼성화재, 한림대병원, LG전자, 그리고 UNIST, 광운대, 경희대, 부산대 교수님들께서 참석해주셨습니다.

또한 9월 25일 오후에는 Wil van der Aalst 교수님께서 Aim 랩 구성원들과 연구 세미나를 진행하였습니다.

Originally published on the AIM Lab board on 2024.10.30.
