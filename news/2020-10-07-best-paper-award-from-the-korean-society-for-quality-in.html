Best Paper Award from the Korean Society for Quality in Health Care(한국의료질향상학회 학술논문상 최우수상 수상)

The paper "Developing data-driven clinical pathways using electronic health records: The cases of total laparoscopic hysterectomy and rotator cuff tears" received the Best Paper Award at the 2020 Korean Society for Quality in Health Care conference.

Paper title: Developing data-driven clinical pathways using electronic health records: The cases of total laparoscopic hysterectomy and rotator cuff tears

Authors: Minsu Cho, Kidong Kim, Jungeun Lim, Hyunyoung Baek, Seok Kim, Hee Hwang, Minseok Song, Sooyoung Yoo
