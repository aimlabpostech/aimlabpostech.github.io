2019 Spring Joint Conference of the Korean Institute of Industrial Engineers

Date: April 10-12, 2019

Venue: Kimdaejung Convention Center (Gwangju)

Attendees: Professor Minseok Song, Dr. Minsu Cho, and M.S. students Gyunam Park, Jungwoo Seo, Deoksang Lee, Sunghee Kim, Woohyun Hwang, and Sinnyeom Park

Paper presentation 1: An FSM-Based Derived Variable Generation Method for Customer Purchase Prediction (Jungwoo Seo)

Paper presentation 2: A Semiconductor Logistics Analysis Method Using a Logistics Data Warehouse (Gyunam Park)

Paper presentation 3: A Study on Blockchain Applications in Healthcare (Deoksang Lee)

Paper presentation 4: A Methodology for Developing Clinical Pathways from Patient Treatment Data Using Conformance Rates (Woohyun Hwang)
