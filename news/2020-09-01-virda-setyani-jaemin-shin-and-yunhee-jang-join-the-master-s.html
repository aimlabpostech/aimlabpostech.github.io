Virda Setyani, Jaemin Shin, and Yunhee Jang Join the Master's Program (Fall 2020)

In the fall semester of 2020, Virda Setyani, Jaemin Shin, and Yunhee Jang were admitted to the master's program, adding three more members to the AIM Lab.

We look forward to a joyful and energetic graduate school life for Virda Setyani, Jaemin Shin, and Yunhee Jang.
