Degree Conferment Ceremony for Graduates Junhyun Park, Youngseok Jang, and Mincheol Jung

This February, graduates Junhyun Park and Mincheol Jung attended the degree conferment ceremony held on February 11.

Graduate Youngseok Jang was unable to attend due to scheduling conflicts.

Our sincere congratulations to all three graduates.
